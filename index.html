Our Story

Brown Lights Media

Brown Lights Media was born in Kozhikode, Kerala, from a shared dream between Ashkar, Nihal, and Ashir. What started as conversations during a memorable trip to Ooty soon became a vision to build a brand driven by creativity and storytelling.

Our journey began with a Sony A7 III, and over the years, our passion has grown into creating timeless photographs and films that preserve life’s most meaningful moments.

We believe every wedding has its own story. That’s why we approach each celebration with a fresh perspective—blending aesthetics, creativity, and thoughtful planning to create visuals that feel authentic, emotional, and unforgettable.

At Brown Lights Media, we’re not just capturing moments—we’re creating memories that last forever.
